Our Journey

Three Decades of Excellence

1994

Foundation

The dR India was founded in Ankleshwar, Gujarat with a small team of 15 professionals focused on providing quality painting services to local chemical industries.

2000

First Major Project

Secured our first major project with UPL Limited, marking the beginning of our expansion into larger industrial coating projects across Gujarat.

2005

Expansion & Certification

Achieved ISO 9001 certification and expanded operations to Mumbai and Ahmedabad, growing our team to over 100 professionals.

2010

Technical Leadership

Became the first company in India to achieve NACE certification for our coating inspectors, establishing our technical leadership in the industry.

2015

International Expansion

Completed our first international project in Saudi Arabia for Farabi Petrochemicals, marking our entry into the global market.

2024

Industry Leadership

Today, with over 600 professionals, 6 offices across India, and recognition as the "Topmost Painting Contractor of India," we continue to lead the industry in innovation and excellence.
