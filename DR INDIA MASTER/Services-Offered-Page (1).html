Common Questions

Service FAQs

Find answers to frequently asked questions about our industrial coating and protection services.

The lifespan of our industrial coatings varies depending on the specific system used, environmental conditions, and substrate type. Typically, our high-performance coating systems provide 10-25 years of protection in standard industrial environments. For severe environments such as chemical processing or offshore applications, specialized systems can still deliver 7-15 years of protection. We provide detailed warranty information and maintenance recommendations for each coating system to maximize service life.

We employ various surface preparation methods based on project requirements, substrate condition, and coating specifications. These include wet-abrasive blasting, dry abrasive blasting, ultra-high pressure water jetting, mechanical preparation (grinding, sanding), chemical cleaning, and hand tool preparation. Our NACE-certified inspectors determine the optimal preparation method to achieve the required surface profile and cleanliness standards according to industry specifications (SSPC/NACE). We prioritize environmentally responsible methods that minimize dust and waste while achieving excellent surface profiles.

Yes, we specialize in executing projects in operational facilities with minimal disruption. Our approach includes careful planning and scheduling to work during planned maintenance periods or off-peak hours, implementing containment systems to prevent dust and overspray from affecting nearby operations, using low-odor and low-VOC coating systems when working in occupied areas, and coordinating closely with facility management to ensure safety protocols are maintained. We've successfully completed numerous projects in active refineries, power plants, and manufacturing facilities while they remained operational.

Safety is our top priority. We implement comprehensive safety measures including detailed site-specific safety plans developed before project commencement, daily safety briefings and toolbox talks for all personnel, proper personal protective equipment (PPE) for all workers, confined space entry protocols when applicable, fall protection systems for elevated work, air monitoring in enclosed spaces, proper ventilation during coating application, and strict compliance with all relevant safety regulations and client-specific requirements. Our safety record exceeds industry standards, and we maintain ISO 45001 certification for occupational health and safety management.

We offer comprehensive warranty options tailored to each project. Our standard workmanship warranty covers application quality for 1-2 years, ensuring that our work meets all specifications and industry standards. For materials, we provide manufacturer-backed warranties ranging from 5-25 years depending on the coating system and application environment. We also offer extended warranty packages that include periodic inspections and maintenance recommendations. All warranties are clearly documented with specific terms and conditions, and we maintain detailed project records to support warranty claims if needed.

Our quality control process is comprehensive and includes developing detailed quality control plans specific to each project, conducting pre-project inspections to establish baseline conditions, performing environmental monitoring during application (temperature, humidity, dew point), using calibrated instruments to verify surface preparation standards (surface profile, cleanliness), measuring wet and dry film thickness throughout application, conducting adhesion testing to verify coating performance, and documenting all quality control activities with detailed reports. Our NACE-certified inspectors oversee the quality control process, ensuring compliance with project specifications and industry standards.
